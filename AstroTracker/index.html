At a glance

Know where every project stands

Integration per filter for every telescope, and each morning a summary of the night before: what every rig captured, and which subs look clouded or trailed.

  • Totals by target, filter and telescope
  • Last night's results, waiting in the morning
  • Subs flagged for clouds and trailing

The record

A complete record for every target

Integration, subs kept, nights and calibration, with the finished picture alongside. Every record in this site's gallery comes straight from here.

  • Goals per filter, and progress towards them
  • Every sub counted once; rejected subs add no time
  • AstroBin acquisition details in one click

Calibration

Catch calibration gaps before they cost a night

Night by night, what each filter collected and whether flats cover it. Missing flats, darks at the wrong temperature and a gain or offset that quietly changed are flagged while there is still time to fix them.

  • Flats paired night by night
  • Darks and bias matched on temperature, gain and offset
  • An alert the moment something slips

Planning

Plan the next clear night

When it gets dark, what the moon allows, and when each target clears your horizon, with a forecast of when every project will be finished.

  • Dark hours and moonlight for every night of the month
  • Which targets suit which telescope's field
  • Checklists: Messier, Caldwell, Sharpless and more

And through the night

Night watch

Follow every rig while it images, on the PC or your phone, with an alert if a sequence stalls or guiding drifts.

Cloud alerts

With an all-sky camera, AstroTracker spots cloud rolling in and lets you know.

Morning summary

What every rig captured overnight, ready when you wake up.

How much more?

Measures the faint detail in your subs and estimates the hours still needed for a clean result.

Tidy files, safely

Sorts subs into folders with a preview first and an undo after. Your image files are never modified.

Weather

The forecast for your site, and a rain alert for rigs left out overnight.

Your data stays yours

AstroTracker runs on your own PC. It only ever reads your image files, never changes them, and nothing leaves your computer unless you switch a feature on.
