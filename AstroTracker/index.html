A tour of the app

From tonight's sky to a finished archive

Each tab below is AstroTracker as it is today, in its Glass look, photographed on the developer's own PC with five rigs set up for the night.

ImagingWeatherPlanningActiveFinishedOrganize

01Imaging

Every rig, live, on one page

Each rig's sequence, its progress per filter, guiding, focus and the latest sub, read from Sequence Generator Pro, N.I.N.A. and PHD2 as they work. With Sequence Generator Pro, start every rig when it gets dark, or the moment the sky clears, and stop and park them all from here.

  • Arm for dark, or for the clear sky your all-sky camera sees
  • Each rig's next meridian flip, counted down
  • Guiding RMS, star size and seeing from every sub
  • Each capture PC's drive space, and Windows Update kept from restarting it mid-sequence
  • An alert the moment a sequence stalls or guiding drifts

02Weather

Know what the sky will do tonight

Cloud cover hour by hour for every dark hour of the week, and a rain alert while rigs are out. Add your own Astrospheric key for seeing and transparency, or an all-sky camera to watch the clouds themselves.

  • Clear dark hours for each night ahead
  • Rain alerts while rigs are imaging or set up
  • All-sky cloud watch, and a ping when it clears
  • Seeing measured from the stars in your own subs

03Planning

Plan the next clear night

When it gets dark, what the moon allows and when each target clears your horizon, for every night of the month. AstroTracker suggests targets you have never imaged, suited to each telescope's field and tonight's moon.

  • A month of nights: dark hours, moonlight, broadband or narrowband
  • Every planned target's altitude through the night
  • Checklists: Messier, Caldwell, Sharpless and more

04Active

Know where every project stands

The Active tab is the morning view: every project in progress, grouped by telescope with its integration per filter, and last night's results above them. What each rig captured, which subs look clouded or trailed, and which filters still need flats.

  • Totals by target, filter and telescope
  • Last night's results, waiting in the morning
  • Flagged subs rejected in one click
  • Each morning, last night's report: a row per rig, and any time lost and why

05Finished

Years of nights, accounted for

Every completed project, telescope by telescope: the hours behind it per filter, the final pictures, and what still sits on a working drive waiting to be archived.

  • Every finished hour, by rig and filter
  • Final pictures kept with their projects
  • What is not archived yet, and the space it takes
  • Set a project Complete, and its archive is offered there and then

06Organize

Tidy files, safely

Your library at a glance, then the work: AstroTracker finds subs filed under the wrong target, strays, and targets sharing a folder, and archives finished projects into one layout. Nothing moves until you tick it and confirm.

  • The whole library's hours, files and copies, with Survey and Tidy my library
  • Misfiled and stray subs found by their headers
  • Finished projects archived; copies set aside, never deleted
  • A preview before, an undo after. Your image files are never modified.

The iPhone app

Your rigs, in your pocket

AstroTracker's iPhone app pairs with AstroTracker on your PC by scanning a code. Its Tonight screen shows every rig at a glance, AstroTracker's own pages are a tab away, and its alerts reach you wherever you are.

  • Tonight: each rig's state and target, the dark hours, the moon and rain
  • Lock screen and home screen widgets, brought up to date by every alert
  • Alerts sealed on your PC, so that only your phone can read them
  • A sound for each kind of alert, and the urgent ones can break through Focus

Coming soon to the App Store

No iPhone? Any phone's browser can open AstroTracker's pages at home, and the free ntfy app takes its alerts.

Inside every project

The whole record, and the calibration behind it

  • Every sub counted once; rejected subs add no time
  • Darks and bias matched on temperature, gain and offset
  • AstroBin acquisition details in one click

And there's more

How much more?

Measures the faint detail in your subs and estimates the hours still needed for a clean result.

Capture PCs

Each capture PC reports its drive space and logs through a folder you already sync, and Windows Update is kept from restarting it at night.

Library survey

Finds your past processing, from WBPP runs to PixInsight projects and finished pictures, and every capture stored twice.

Your look

Glass or Classic, and a loading screen of your choice: a telescope locking on, a galaxy coming out of the noise, and more.

Rigs by fingerprint

Each rig is recognised by focal length, pixel size and sensor, whatever the capture program calls it.

Backups

AstroTracker backs up its own record to a folder you choose, and checks every copy it makes.

Your data stays yours

AstroTracker runs on your own PC. It only ever reads your image files, never changes them, and nothing leaves your computer unless you switch a feature on.
