Getting help with AstroTracker

AstroTracker is in beta, and I answer every question myself. Write to me, or ask the community on Discord.

Email brandon.lewis@blastrophoto.com Join the Discord

Something went wrong

Save a problem report and email it with a line about what happened. In AstroTracker, open Settings › This PC › Support and press Save problem report. It saves a zip file on your PC with the log, your settings, your library's folder layout, your rigs and the current alerts. No images and no database go in it, and your licence key, pairing code and any password are taken out before it is written. It is an ordinary zip: open it and look before you send it.

AstroTracker never sends a report by itself.

Learning your way around

AstroTracker Explained is a friendly guide to everything AstroTracker does, and why, written for anyone new to astrophotography. It is also a PDF to keep. Inside the app, Guides at the top of every page explains the parts that touch other machines: capture PCs, logs, the all-sky camera and your phone.

The iPhone app

The iPhone app is a companion to AstroTracker on your PC, and works only with it. It is coming soon to the App Store.

What it needs: an iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and AstroTracker 0.35.18 or later on your PC. The newest AstroTracker is best, and it offers its updates by itself.

  • Pairing. On the PC, open Settings › General › Phone access and allow phones on this network (it needs a restart of AstroTracker, and Windows asks once to allow it on private networks). In the app, choose Pair with your PC and scan the code the PC shows.
  • Notifications. Allow them when the app asks. They reach your phone wherever you are, sealed on your PC so that only your phone can read them. In the app, This phone › Sounds picks a sound for each kind of alert.
  • Tonight and the pages read your PC directly, so they need your phone on your home network, or on your own VPN.
  • A phone you no longer use can be removed under Settings › General › Phone access on the PC, or with Unpair in the app; your other phones stay paired. What the app keeps, and how it is removed, is on the privacy page.
  • No PC to pair with yet? See a demo night opens the whole app on a real night recorded inside it.

Your data

AstroTracker runs on your own PC and reads your image files without ever changing them. What it keeps, and the few things that ever leave your computer, are listed in full on the privacy page.
